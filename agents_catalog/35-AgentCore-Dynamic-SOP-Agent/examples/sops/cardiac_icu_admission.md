# Cardiac ICU Patient Admission Protocol

**SOP ID:** SOP-CARD-001  
**Category:** Clinical  
**Priority:** High  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** 2024-12-10  
**Applicable Departments:** Cardiology, ICU, Emergency  
**Applicable Roles:** Physician, Nurse, Critical Care Specialist

## Overview

This SOP defines the standardized procedure for admitting patients to the Cardiac Intensive Care Unit (ICU). The protocol ensures comprehensive assessment, proper monitoring setup, and complete documentation.

## Prerequisites

- ICU bed availability confirmed
- Attending physician order received
- Patient consent obtained
- Emergency equipment ready

## Procedure

### Step 1: Verify Patient Identity and Orders

**Description:** Confirm patient identity and admission orders

You MUST verify the patient's identity using at least two identifiers (name and date of birth or medical record number). You MUST confirm the attending physician's admission orders and ensure all required documentation is complete.

**Validation Criteria:**
- Patient identity confirmed with two identifiers
- Admission orders signed by attending physician
- All consent forms completed

**Warnings:**
- Never proceed without proper patient identification
- Ensure all allergies are documented in the system

**Estimated Time:** 5 minutes

---

### Step 2: Perform Initial Assessment

**Description:** Conduct comprehensive patient assessment

You MUST perform a complete cardiovascular assessment including vital signs, cardiac rhythm, respiratory status, and neurological status. You SHOULD document baseline laboratory values and imaging results. You MAY consult with the cardiology fellow if clinical status is unclear.

**Validation Criteria:**
- All vital signs recorded
- Cardiac rhythm documented
- Baseline labs reviewed
- Physical examination completed

**Warnings:**
- Report any signs of acute deterioration immediately
- Notify physician of abnormal vital signs

**Estimated Time:** 15 minutes

---

### Step 3: Set Up Cardiac Monitoring

**Description:** Establish continuous cardiac and hemodynamic monitoring

You MUST connect the patient to continuous telemetry monitoring. You MUST establish arterial line monitoring if ordered. You SHOULD set appropriate alarm limits based on patient condition. You MAY initiate additional hemodynamic monitoring (Swan-Ganz catheter, ScvO2) per physician orders.

**Validation Criteria:**
- Telemetry monitoring active with clear waveforms
- Alarm limits appropriately set
- All monitoring devices functioning properly
- Backup monitoring available

**Warnings:**
- Test all alarms before leaving bedside
- Ensure monitoring equipment is properly calibrated

**Estimated Time:** 10 minutes

---

### Step 4: Review and Administer Medications

**Description:** Verify medication orders and initiate therapy

You MUST perform medication reconciliation comparing home medications with admission orders. You MUST verify all medication allergies. You SHOULD initiate time-critical medications within specified timeframes. You MAY hold medications if contraindicated by current clinical status, but MUST notify the physician.

**Validation Criteria:**
- Medication reconciliation completed
- All allergies verified and documented
- Critical medications administered on time
- Medication administration documented

**Warnings:**
- Double-check all high-risk medications (anticoagulants, vasopressors, inotropes)
- Never administer medications with documented allergies

**Estimated Time:** 20 minutes

---

### Step 5: Complete Documentation

**Description:** Document admission and initial care in medical record

You MUST complete the ICU admission note including history, assessment, and plan. You MUST document all procedures performed and medications administered. You SHOULD complete the ICU flowsheet with initial data. You MAY use templates but MUST customize to patient-specific information.

**Validation Criteria:**
- Admission note completed in EHR
- All procedures documented
- Flowsheet initiated with baseline data
- Care plan documented and communicated

**Warnings:**
- Ensure documentation is completed within 2 hours of admission
- Include all clinically relevant information

**Estimated Time:** 20 minutes

---

### Step 6: Family Communication and Handoff

**Description:** Communicate with family and complete nursing handoff

You MUST provide family with ICU orientation and visiting policy. You SHOULD explain the monitoring equipment and care plan. You MUST complete structured handoff to oncoming staff if shift change occurs. You MAY involve social work or chaplaincy if family requests support.

**Validation Criteria:**
- Family orientation completed
- Questions addressed
- Contact information obtained
- Handoff communication documented

**Estimated Time:** 15 minutes
